NestChat Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

NestChat is a private messaging app built for a single household. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and who can see it.

Who this app is for

NestChat is not a general public product. It's designed to be installed only by members of one household. Anyone who signs in becomes a visible member of that household's roster to everyone else who has signed in — there is no per-user data isolation between household members.

Information we collect

  • Phone number. You type in a phone number when you sign in. It is stored against your profile but is not verified — no SMS code is sent, and anyone can type any number.
  • Profile info. Display name and, if you add one, a profile photo.
  • Messages and media. Text messages, photos, videos, and documents you send are stored so they can be delivered to and displayed for other members of a chat.
  • Location, only when you choose to share it. If you use the "Share Live Location" feature, your device's location is sent and stored for the duration you choose (15 minutes, 24 hours, or until you stop it), and is visible to the members of that specific chat. Location is not otherwise collected.
  • Device contacts, for on-device matching only. If you grant contacts permission, the app reads your device's contact list locally to show which of your contacts are already on NestChat. Your device contacts are never uploaded to or stored on our servers.
  • Presence and read receipts. Online/offline status and message read status are shared with other members by default; you can turn this off in Settings.
  • Push notification token. If you allow notifications, a device token is stored so the app can notify you of new messages.

Who can see your information

Every person who has signed into this specific household's NestChat instance can see the full member roster (display name and phone number) and can start a chat with any other member. Message content is only visible to members of the specific chat it was sent in.

How information is stored

Data is stored using Supabase (Postgres database, file storage, and authentication). Messages, media, and location data persist until deleted. You can delete individual messages you sent (soft delete) and household admins can remove a member's account.

Third parties

We use the following service providers to operate the app:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and file storage.
  • Expo / Apple Push Notification service — delivering push notifications.

We do not sell or share your information with advertisers or data brokers. There are no ads in this app.

Data retention and deletion

Messages and media are retained until deleted. Status updates expire automatically after 24 hours. To have your account and associated data removed, contact the household admin who invited you, or email the contact below.

Children

This app is not directed at children and is not available on the public App Store — it is distributed only to members of one household via TestFlight.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to the app's developer at the contact information provided in your TestFlight invitation.
